# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 24, 2026**

Pranta Dutta ("we", "our", or "us") operates The Accountant mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Email address
- Display name
- Basic profile information provided when you choose to sign in with a third-party identity provider (such as Google or Apple)

When you sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address, in which case we receive a private relay email address rather than your personal one.

### 1.2 Financial Data
The App allows you to record and manage:
- Transaction records (amounts, descriptions, dates, categories)
- Wallet and account information
- Budgets, goals, subscriptions, and credit/debt records
- Currency and display preferences

**Important:** Your financial data is stored locally on your device in a database. If you choose to enable cloud sync (a Premium feature), your data is transmitted securely to our servers for backup and cross-device synchronization only.

### 1.3 Device & Usage Information
We may collect:
- Device type and operating system version
- App usage analytics (screens visited, features used)
- Crash reports and diagnostic logs
- Push notification tokens

### 1.4 Receipt & Image Data
If you use the receipt scanning feature:
- Text recognition is performed **on your device** using an on-device machine-learning library
- Receipt images are not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly enable cloud backup
- Extracted text is stored locally with your transaction records

### 1.5 AI Feature Data
If you use Premium AI features (such as AI insights or the AI assistant), the content you submit for those features — for example your questions and a summary of the relevant financial context — is transmitted to our third-party AI provider (Google Gemini) solely to generate a response. We do not use this content to train third-party models. If you do not use these features, no such data is transmitted.

### 1.6 Purchase Information
When you buy a subscription or one-time purchase, the transaction is processed by the app marketplace through which you downloaded the App. **We do not receive or store your full payment card details.** We receive a purchase receipt or token, which we and our subscription-verification service use to confirm, restore, and manage your entitlement.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the App's core functionality
- Authenticate your identity and secure your account
- Verify and manage purchases and subscriptions
- Synchronize your data across devices (when enabled)
- Send notifications about your budgets, bills, and account (with your permission)
- Generate AI-powered insights (Premium feature)
- Improve the App's performance and user experience
- Respond to support requests

## 3. Legal Bases (EEA/UK Users)

Where the GDPR applies, we process your data on the following bases: performance of our contract with you (to provide the App), your consent (for optional analytics and notifications), and our legitimate interests (to secure and improve the App).

## 4. Data Storage & Security

### 4.1 Local Storage
- Financial data is stored locally on your device
- Authentication tokens are stored in secure device storage (platform keychain/keystore)
- We use industry-standard encryption to protect data in transit

### 4.2 Cloud Sync
- Cloud sync is optional and can be enabled or disabled at any time
- Synced data is transmitted over HTTPS with TLS encryption
- Server-side data is stored in secured databases with access controls

### 4.3 Data Retention
- Local data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data
- Cloud-synced data is retained while your account is active
- Upon account deletion, associated cloud-stored data is permanently removed within 30 days

## 6. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) to protect your information during these transfers.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Export** your financial data at any time
- **Correct** inaccurate information
- **Delete** your account and associated data
- **Opt out** of analytics collection
- **Disable** cloud sync to keep all data local-only
- **Withdraw consent** for push notifications

If you are in the EEA/UK, you also have the right to object to or restrict processing and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell your personal information.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at prantadutta1997@gmail.com.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under the applicable age, we will delete it.
