# Refund Policy

**Last updated: July 24, 2026**

This Refund Policy explains how refunds work for purchases made in The Accountant mobile application (the "App"), operated by Pranta Dutta ("we", "our", or "us"). Please read it together with our Terms of Use.

## 1. How Purchases Are Processed

All subscriptions and one-time purchases in the App are sold and processed by the app marketplace from which you downloaded the App. We do not directly process payments and we do not have access to your payment card details.

Because the marketplace is the seller of record, **refunds are handled by the app marketplace in accordance with its own refund policy**, not directly by us.

## 2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund for a purchase or subscription, please submit your request through the app marketplace you used to buy it, using its standard refund or purchase-support process (typically found in your account's purchase history or order/receipt details).

Refund eligibility, time limits, and outcomes are determined solely by the marketplace under its policies and applicable law.

## 3. Subscriptions

- **Cancelling a subscription** stops future renewals but does not, by itself, refund the current billing period. You retain access to Premium features until the end of the period you already paid for.
- To cancel, open your marketplace account's subscription settings and turn off the subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.
- Managing a subscription (cancel, upgrade, downgrade) is done entirely through your marketplace account settings.

## 4. One-Time ("Lifetime") Purchases

A one-time "lifetime" purchase is non-recurring. Refund requests for one-time purchases are also handled by the app marketplace under its policies.

## 5. Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable refund or cancellation rights you may have under the consumer protection laws of your country or region. Where such laws apply, they take precedence over this policy.

## 6. Restoring Purchases

If you reinstall the App or switch to a new device using the same marketplace account, you can restore your existing Premium entitlement at no additional charge using the App's "Restore Purchases" option. This restores access you already paid for and is not a new charge or a refund.
